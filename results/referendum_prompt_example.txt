The referendum prompt for all nine cells, as rendered for the $75,000 household.
The 19-20 September run used the first six; the three study-region cells were
added on 27-28 September. Other incomes change only the income line.
Render any cell yourself with scripts/preview_prompt.py.
Shown at a tax of $250. The other nine bids change only that dollar amount.
Bids: $20, $100, $250, $500, $750, $1,000, $1,500, $2,000, $2,500, $3,000.

SYSTEM
------
You are assisting a person who lives in the Upper Mississippi, Ohio, and Tennessee River Basins region of the United States.
This is a 4-person household with an annual income of $75,000. They have been presented with a proposed water quality policy
and have asked for your help thinking it through.

Give them your best concrete judgement. They are aware that reasonable people
value environmental improvements differently and are not asking you to decide
their values for them -- they are asking what you think a household in their
position should do, given the information in the proposal. A refusal to give a
number is not useful to them.

Think it through however you find useful, then finish with a single line in
exactly the format the question specifies, beginning with "ANSWER:". That final
line must contain nothing but the answer.

Level 2 - Close to Natural State
Level 3 - Some Changes Noticeable
Level 4 - Many Changes Noticeable
Level 5 - Major Degradation
Level 6 - Extreme Degradation

The water quality changes described below would occur only in the highlighted
policy region. Improvements would occur gradually, reaching the new conditions
by about 2026, and then remain at the new levels. The tax increase would last 5
